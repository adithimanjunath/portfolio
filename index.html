About

The short version.

I grew up thinking of software as something you use, not something you make. That changed in my B.E., and by the end of my Master's in UX Engineering, I was building the interfaces I'd wanted my whole life.

The QA years shaped how I ship: I'd rather catch a broken flow in a test than in a user complaint. The UX years shaped how I design: I'd rather remove three fields than add one help text. Together they mean I take a while to open a PR, and the PRs are usually clean.

Outside work I lecture at SRH Heidelberg, go to gym, and try new cafés!
